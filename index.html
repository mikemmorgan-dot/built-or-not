    What's counted. Every project on the Major Projects Office's referred list (the North Coast Transmission Line's three phases as one) and every transformative strategy, plus projects publicly reported from the September Canada Investment Summit that name a proponent and a location. The Summit's project prospectus is not public, so that list is not exhaustive.

    Stages. Concept: no regulatory filing. Under assessment: a review is open. Approved: a key federal or provincial approval issued, no construction yet. Under construction: physical work verified. "National interest" means listed in Schedule 1 of the Building Canada Act; a referral to the MPO is not a designation.

    Capital. One figure per project: the most recent from the proponent or a government, at the low end of any range. Figures stated in US dollars (Crawford, Matawinie, McIlvenna Bay, Prairie Connector) are converted at the average Bank of Canada USD/CAD rate from January 2 to October 14, 2026, not the rate on the day each was announced. Strategy-level figures are aggregates or public funding and are left out.

    Spending. Only spending reported in a filing or by a government counts. A final investment decision is committed capital, not spent capital. Darlington's figure is life-to-date to June 30. Not financial advice; no forecasts.
